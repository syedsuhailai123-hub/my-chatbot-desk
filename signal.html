This shows what the frozen rule says on the latest completed hourly candle. It is information only, not an instruction to trade. The rule has not yet passed its forward test. The demo bot places orders only when it is run manually with the trade flag.
How to read this page

A LONG or SHORT signal means every condition for that direction is passing on the last completed candle. FRESH means the candle started within the last 3 hours, which the demo bot requires before acting. STALE means the rule fired but the candle is too old to act on. MARKET CLOSED means gold is not trading right now (roughly Friday 22:00 to Sunday 22:00 UTC). If a signal fires, the rule would enter at the next candle's open, not at the price shown here.
