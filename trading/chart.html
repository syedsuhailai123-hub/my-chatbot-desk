How this works

  • Candles are hourly prices from the market data source, refreshed every hour.
  • Amber and blue lines are the 20 and 50 period averages. The rule uses them to judge trend.
  • Triangles mark moments when the frozen rule would have signalled a long (green, up) or short (red, down) trade.
  • Circles are predictions you recorded. Green means it was a hit, red a miss, white still pending.
  • Scoring compares every graded prediction with the baseline: how often price rose over the same window in the past. A hit rate only means something if it beats the baseline over 100+ graded predictions.
  • What it cannot do: it does not predict prices. It shows evidence, and it keeps score honestly.
