How to read it
DEMO ORDER PLACED: the demo bot sent an order for this candle and it filled. NO DEMO ORDER: the rule fired on a fresh candle, but no order is recorded yet. Run python3 publish_demo.py on your Mac after the bot runs, then refresh. NO TRADE: no signal, market closed, or the signal was too old. UNKNOWN: the candle was not recorded live, so the bot's action can't be known. Demo orders are practice-account orders only.
